## Ground rules — Kay's non-negotiables (confirmed 2026-09-26; the motion pass of 2026-10-01 changed 1, 3, 4 and 5 with her yes). Do not change these without asking her first.

1. **Camera = Léo (leoparpeix.com).** Position is a *linear* function of page scroll (`camYAt(s) = -RATE * s`, `s = scroll / 100vh`), everywhere, even while paper covers the world. Rotation is fixed forever (`CAMERA.pitch`, no yaw, no mouse orbit). The header dollies down 2.0 and forward 4 over its 200vh. The only smoothing is Lenis (lerp .1). It clamps just after the landing: it sinks `TIGER.glb.settle` further (`settleMobile` on phones) so the grass and the whole sitting tiger are in frame. Before any scroll there is the opening (Kay, 2026-09-27, replacing the 000→100 loader; a film opening in beats since 2026-10-01): a title card (her name, set like a credit: `COPY.nav.sub` / `name` / `tagline`) holds while the tiger loads → it folds away and the tiger drops onto the paper out of its own contact shadow (a close shot) → click / tap / scroll → it waves → a line is drawn down the paper through the tiger, the paper splits along it and both halves slide off while the camera backs out to the rail's first position (the world shows only inside the split; the tiger stays in front of the paper: it and the lights are on layer 1, `renderOpening` draws it alone over the paper and the world scissored to the gap) → the nav's words, the statement and the scroll hint rise in that order and the tiger nods down the ladder twice; then scrolling starts (`ENTRY` / `entryTick` in `app.js`, timings `OPENING` in `content.js`; phases card → cardOut → drop → void → wave → enter → done; a click or scroll before the tiger has landed is kept and skips the wave; `?snap` skips it unless `&entry=1`; `node merge/tools/opening.js` steps through it). A click before the GLB has loaded waits for it (the card holds, up to 12 s; the placeholder rig never shows in the opening); the page and nav are `inert` until the opening starts; a reload always starts at the top (`history.scrollRestoration = 'manual'`). One exception (Kay, 2026-09-26): in front of the computer on floor 03 the camera holds still for (projects − 1) screens while the previews slide, so `camYAt` is piecewise-linear with that one plateau (Léo's camera also holds under his long papers).
2. **Tiger = Laurens (laurens.art).** The tiger keeps one place on the right of the frame (hips glued to the camera: `hips.y = camera.y - LAY.glue`). Every locomotion state is *scrubbed by scroll* — `idle → turnToWall → overEdge → climbing (loop) → landing → turnAround → sit` in `app.js`, each a pure function of the scroll phase. Only idle bits (breathing, blink, ears, tail, the wave) run on time. It glances at each project as it arrives and looks back over its shoulder on hover/click. It climbs one ladder from the top platform to the meadow through every floor. Since 2026-09-27 the tiger is Kay's own model (Tripo → Mixamo): `assets/tiger/tiger.glb` with one clip per state, `action.time` set from the same scroll phases (the climb clip is the Mixamo climb-up played backwards, its time a function of the hips height so planted feet stay on rungs). The procedural `Rig` is the fallback until the GLB loads, if it fails, or with `?rig`. The GLB tiger has no blink, ear or tail rig; its breathing is in the idle clips. The one exception to scroll-scrubbing is the meadow's talent show (Kay, 2026-09-27): it runs on time and may take the tiger off its spot. The catwalk's heading and length are planned when it starts (`planWalk`, with the live camera: hips clear of the pier and rails, the whole body in frame, off the shore words on wide screens; on phones the walk is shortened). Scrolling away mid-act eases it out in 0.4 s.
3. **Paper / world alternation (Léo).** DOM paper blocks (hero, the floors' full-screen title blocks, footer) scroll 1:1 over the WebGL world; the world is visible in the "windows". Never two white blocks in a row (Kay, 2026-09-26): the value statements are not paper — each is revealed char by char over the room at the end of its window (Léo's library statement), paper-coloured in dark rooms; since 2026-10-01 each floor says it its own way (`FLOOR_STYLE.value`): the workshop stamps the letters on, the bar's flicker on like neon (and keep a glow), the computer types them behind a block cursor. Since 2026-09-27 the intro is a window too: the tiger leans off the ladder toward you and says Kay's intro lines in a see-through game dialogue bubble, page by page with the scroll, each page typed out (`COPY.intro.pages`; `COPY.intro.photo` = a picture of Kay shown on hover, none yet). The archives are see-through (88% paper + blur, darker secondary ink for contrast), not a white block. Statements reveal char by char; other text uses masked line reveals (SplitText) with the `reveal` ease `cubic-bezier(.4,0,0,1)`.
4. **Projects are frameless.** No card, no border, no background. Each project is its cut-out object floating in the room (`.card figure`), with a soft drop shadow; its number and name announce themselves beside it (masked rise) when it reaches the middle of the screen (Kay, 2026-10-01; `.card.on`), the rest of the words come *beside* it on hover/focus in plain ink (or paper on dark floors; always on phones). Its entrance is scrubbed by scroll (`cardFrame`): it rises out of its shadow, turns a little as it passes, sinks on the way out, and the ones not in the middle step back. Each floor shows its work its own way (`FLOOR_STYLE.show`): the workshop prints the part out of its line drawing, bottom up behind an orange print head (`<slug>-line.webp` from `merge/tools/lines.py`; `lineImg` on the piece), the bar finds each object in the dark with a spotlight that clacks on, the computer's previews step back when not in front and the screen powers on like a CRT as the floor arrives. Click/Enter opens the detail panel, which slides in from the left while the tiger stays visible on the right; the object itself flies into the panel (into the write-up's cover when that is the same cut-out, else `figure.obj` at the panel's top right) and back to its spot on close (`fly`).
5. **Three floors.** `SECTIONS` in `content.js`: 01 ENGINEERING (workshop, light) → 02 DESIGN & INTERACTION (night bar, dark) → 03 ANALYTICS & STRATEGY (one big beige desktop computer, dark). Rhythm per section: the title block (`TITLE_SCREENS` = 1: a full-screen paper block, compact type — Kay, 2026-10-01; she tried and dropped elevator doors the same day) → the room with its projects → the value statement over the room. The floor change happens behind the paper: the camera passes the slab (which stops 2.7 in front of the lens, with a hatch for the ladder) at `crossS` (the block covering the screen), the room's skin blends across ±0.35 screens (`themeAt`), and as the paper lifts the new room is dark (`LIGHTS.dark` × its ambient light): its lamps and glowing props click on one by one (`LIGHTS.on`, after the paper has gone; neon stutters, bulbs pop), and every lamp further down stays off until it rises into the frame, so the whole descent keeps lighting up (`lampK`; scrolling back switches them off). The lift's display (`#lift`, bottom left, `liftFrame`) shows the floor number (it rolls at each slab), the floor's name and the depth (the camera's y), from floor 1's title block to the archives. On floor 03 the projects live on the computer's screen (`deck: true`): a horizontal strip of previews on a fixed DOM overlay (`#deck`) laid over the projected 3D glass every frame; page scroll slides it one preview per screen during the camera hold; ← → keys, horizontal trackpad and drag also move it; click opens the same detail panel. The desk has one leg, at its far left, and rests against the pier, so the meadow under it stays open for the landing and the talent show. Title blocks (`.paper`, z-index 4) cover the computer's overlay (`#deck`, z-index 3). Nav and project text flip to paper colour inside dark rooms (Léo's rule); on the landed meadow the nav splits (`nav-split`): brand and links in paper over the dark wall, the right-hand links in ink over the pier.
6. **Ending.** After the third floor: the archives (see-through) → the meadow window (`MEADOW_SCREENS`; the landing is `LAND_AT` screens into it): the sky flips light, "THE OTHER SHORE" arrives with the landing and stays pinned (`--pin`, the camera is clamped), the tiger steps off the ladder a little left and forward onto the grass (`TIGER.glb.xGround`, `zGround`: the stage, clear of the pier and the rails; grass and flowers keep off it), turns to face you and sits; its gaze follows the cursor; "psst — click me" (not on phones, where it covered the button); clicking it, or the "Ask for a trick" button beside Say hi (`#trick`, the keyboard way in), opens the talent show (Dance / Zombie / Catwalk, `PERF` in `app.js`, only on the meadow; a group of buttons, Escape closes it and focus goes back) → then the footer paper. Clicking or tapping the tiger casts a ray from the click itself (window listener), so it works through the paper/window sections and on phones.
7. **The visitor is rewarded (Kay, 2026-10-01).** Progress lives in localStorage `kooky.progress` (`PROG` in `app.js`: projects seen, achievements said, what the tiger wears, visits, last floor, seconds here; never in the Studio's preview). Opening a project marks it seen (`markSeen`); the lift display shows `n / 13 seen` and the minutes here. Seen counts unlock the talent show's acts (`COPY.talents.acts[][3]`: Dance 0, Zombie 4, Catwalk 8, Thumbs up 13 — the hidden fourth act, clip `thumbs`) and the wardrobe (`WEAR` in `content.js`, `need` per piece: Kay models each piece on `assets/src/wear/tiger-ref.glb` (the tiger's bind pose, metres, Y up, exported by `tools/tiger_ref.py`), exports it alone with transforms applied to `assets/wear/<id>.glb`, and `wearItem` lands it on its bone (`bone`: Head / Neck / Spine1) through the bind pose captured in `setupTiger` (`GT.bind`); `src: null` = not delivered, shown as "soon"; pieces are chosen in the Wardrobe menu beside Ask for a trick on the meadow and worn everywhere, kept between visits). Achievements are mono toasts (`#toast`, `COPY.progress.ach`: first project, a floor cleared, half, all, the landing), each once. A return visit gets "oh. you again." and a toast naming the last floor. Staying ≥ `REWARD.stay` s on a centred project makes the tiger nod (clip `nod`, head-only layer over the climb: `climbBody` / `climbHead` / `nodHead`) with a quip; rushing a project past the middle in < `REWARD.rush` s gets a look-away (`lookaway`), rate-limited. The cursor also drives the world (`REWARD.cursor`, `REWARD.light`): in the dark rooms the viewer's lamp (`camLight`) sits where the pointer points on the wall or the pier (C-a); the tiger's head follows the pointer while it stands or climbs (C-b, `T.fol` on `headLook`); on the meadow the grass bends away from the pointer's spot and the flowers nod (C-d, `grassFrame`, instanced matrices); a light shows through the big words near the pointer (C-g, `.glow` / `.glow-c`, `glowFrame`). Everything cursor-driven is off on phones.
8. **Copy is truthful.** `content.js` holds only facts Kay verified. Where a project has a `detail.todo`, that field lists what is still missing — never invent roles, numbers, tools or outcomes. The project count drives every "thirteen" in the copy (`{N}` templates).
9. **Scope discipline.** Change only what Kay asks for. Before a layout-level change, restate what will and will not change and get a yes.

## Workflow

- **Preview the source**: `cd merge && python3 -m http.server 8000` → http://localhost:8000/preview.html. `?snap=1` fixes the frame step (dt .2) for screenshots.
- **Build**: `cd merge && python3 build.py . dist`, then `cp dist/index.html ../index.html` (Pages serves the root). Commit both.
- **Check motion**: `npm i -D playwright && npx playwright install chromium` once, then `POS="0:h0,1.1:edge,4.3:climb,L:land,L0.7:sit" node merge/tools/shot.js` → `merge/shots/*.png` (`L` = the landing screen). `node merge/tools/errcheck.js` for console/page errors. Look at the frames — the tiger's limbs must move between neighbouring scroll positions; the camera must not turn.
- **Tiger GLB**: `B=/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender; G=assets/src/tiger/gestures; $B -b --factory-startup -P merge/tools/tiger_anim.py -- build "$G/_packs/Gestures Pack Basic/tiger-mixamo.fbx" $G assets/src/tiger/tiger-raw.glb`, then `npx gltf-transform resize … --width 1024 --height 1024`, `webp`, `resample`, `prune`, `dedup` → `assets/tiger/tiger.glb` (~1.2 MB). Tripo FBXs must be imported with image search off (their texture paths point at Tripo's server). A fresh build has Tripo's beige back again, so then paint it: `node merge/tools/tiger_tex.mjs extract assets/tiger/tiger.glb /tmp/tt`, `python3 merge/tools/tiger_paint.py /tmp/tt/mesh.json assets/src/tiger/tiger-mixamo-tex/tiger_basecolor.jpg /tmp/tt/paint.png`, `node merge/tools/tiger_tex.mjs swap assets/tiger/tiger.glb /tmp/tt/paint.png assets/tiger/tiger.glb`, and look at `VIEWS=climb node merge/tools/tiger-turntable.js assets/tiger/tiger.glb merge/shots/tiger.png`.
- **Cut-outs**: `pip3 install rembg onnxruntime pillow scipy opencv-python`, drop photos in `assets/projects/`, run `cutout.py`, then set `img: 'assets/projects/<slug>.webp'` on the piece in `content.js`.
- **Write-ups / the Studio**: Kay double-clicks `merge/studio/Open Studio.command` (or `python3 merge/studio/server.py`) → http://localhost:8010. She edits and says "studio updated"; Claude starts each round with `python3 merge/studio/claude.py changes` (her edits vs the last look, answers, notes), acts (edit the JSON files directly — the Studio notices within 4 s), then `claude.py mark`. Every fact in a write-up cites a Drive source; what isn't in a source becomes a question in the block, never a sentence. Research drafts land in `writeups-private/drafts/` and come in with `claude.py import <slug>` (won't replace blocks Kay touched). **Going live**: Kay sets a project to ● Approved in the Studio → `writeups.js` updates → build → commit → push (only the approved project's JSON and its pictures, never `writeups-private/`, `data/profile.json` or client material she hasn't cleared).
- **The Drive**: Kay's `KAY-PORTFOLIO` folder is mounted at `~/Library/CloudStorage/GoogleDrive-kaytu2027@u.northwestern.edu/My Drive/KAY-PORTFOLIO` (shortcuts to course team folders resolve with `find -L`). `.gdoc`/`.gslides`/`.gsheet` files are stubs holding a `doc_id` → read them with the Drive MCP `read_file_content`. PDFs/HEIC/pptx/videos are real files (`merge/tools/pics.py`). No people in pictures (never patients/users/minors).
- **Deploy**: commit to `main` and `git push origin main`; Pages rebuilds in about a minute. Kay pushes herself (SSH key).
- Commit messages: end with a `Co-Authored-By: Claude …` line.
