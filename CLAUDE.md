## Ground rules — Kay's non-negotiables (confirmed 2026-09-26). Do not change these without asking her first.

1. **Camera = Léo (leoparpeix.com).** Position is a *linear* function of page scroll (`camYAt(s) = -RATE * s`, `s = scroll / 100vh`), everywhere, even while paper covers the world. Rotation is fixed forever (`CAMERA.pitch`, no yaw, no mouse orbit). The header dollies down 2.0 and forward 4 over its 200vh. The only smoothing is Lenis (lerp .1). It clamps just after the landing: it sinks `TIGER.glb.settle` further (`settleMobile` on phones) so the grass and the whole sitting tiger are in frame. Before any scroll there is the opening (Kay, 2026-09-27; it replaced the 000→100 loader): the tiger alone on paper, a close shot; click / tap / scroll → it waves → the camera backs out to the rail's first position while a paper fog lifts, so the world appears from near to far; then scrolling starts (`ENTRY` in `app.js`; `?snap` skips it unless `&entry=1`). A click before the GLB has loaded waits for it (phase `waiting`; the placeholder rig never shows in the opening); the page and nav are `inert` until the opening starts; a reload always starts at the top (`history.scrollRestoration = 'manual'`). One exception (Kay, 2026-09-26): in front of the computer on floor 03 the camera holds still for (projects − 1) screens while the previews slide, so `camYAt` is piecewise-linear with that one plateau (Léo's camera also holds under his long papers).
2. **Tiger = Laurens (laurens.art).** The tiger keeps one place on the right of the frame (hips glued to the camera: `hips.y = camera.y - LAY.glue`). Every locomotion state is *scrubbed by scroll* — `idle → turnToWall → overEdge → climbing (loop) → landing → turnAround → sit` in `app.js`, each a pure function of the scroll phase. Only idle bits (breathing, blink, ears, tail, the wave) run on time. It glances at each project as it arrives and looks back over its shoulder on hover/click. It climbs one ladder from the top platform to the meadow through every floor. Since 2026-09-27 the tiger is Kay's own model (Tripo → Mixamo): `assets/tiger/tiger.glb` with one clip per state, `action.time` set from the same scroll phases (the climb clip is the Mixamo climb-up played backwards, its time a function of the hips height so planted feet stay on rungs). The procedural `Rig` is the fallback until the GLB loads, if it fails, or with `?rig`. The GLB tiger has no blink, ear or tail rig; its breathing is in the idle clips. The one exception to scroll-scrubbing is the meadow's talent show (Kay, 2026-09-27): it runs on time and may take the tiger off its spot. The catwalk's heading and length are planned when it starts (`planWalk`, with the live camera: hips clear of the pier and rails, the whole body in frame, off the shore words on wide screens; on phones the walk is shortened). Scrolling away mid-act eases it out in 0.4 s.
3. **Paper / world alternation (Léo).** DOM paper blocks (hero, section title bands, footer) scroll 1:1 over the WebGL world; the world is visible in the "windows". Never two white blocks in a row (Kay, 2026-09-26): the value statements are not paper — each is revealed char by char over the room at the end of its window (Léo's library statement), paper-coloured in dark rooms. Since 2026-09-27 the intro is a window too: the tiger leans off the ladder toward you and says Kay's intro lines in a see-through game dialogue bubble, page by page with the scroll, each page typed out (`COPY.intro.pages`; `COPY.intro.photo` = a picture of Kay shown on hover, none yet). The archives are see-through (88% paper + blur, darker secondary ink for contrast), not a white block. Statements reveal char by char; other text uses masked line reveals (SplitText) with the `reveal` ease `cubic-bezier(.4,0,0,1)`.
4. **Projects are frameless.** No card, no border, no background. Each project is its cut-out object floating in the room (`.card figure`), with a soft drop shadow; the words appear *beside* it on hover in plain ink (or paper on dark floors). Click/Enter opens the detail panel, which slides in from the left while the tiger stays visible on the right.
5. **Three floors.** `SECTIONS` in `content.js`: 01 ENGINEERING (workshop, light) → 02 DESIGN & INTERACTION (night bar, dark) → 03 ANALYTICS & STRATEGY (one big beige desktop computer, dark). Rhythm per section: title band (`TITLE_SCREENS` = 0.6 of a screen, compact type — Kay: a full screen was too big) → the room with its projects → the value statement over the room. The floor change is seen, like an elevator: the camera passes the slab (which stops 2.7 in front of the lens, with a hatch for the ladder) when the title band is centred (`crossS`), and the room's skin blends across ±0.35 screens of scroll (`themeAt`). On floor 03 the projects live on the computer's screen (`deck: true`): a horizontal strip of previews on a fixed DOM overlay (`#deck`) laid over the projected 3D glass every frame; page scroll slides it one preview per screen during the camera hold; ← → keys, horizontal trackpad and drag also move it; click opens the same detail panel. The desk has one leg, at its far left, and rests against the pier, so the meadow under it stays open for the landing and the talent show. Title bands (`.paper`, z-index 4) cover the computer's overlay (`#deck`, z-index 3). Nav and project text flip to paper colour inside dark rooms (Léo's rule); on the landed meadow the nav splits (`nav-split`): brand and links in paper over the dark wall, the right-hand links in ink over the pier.
6. **Ending.** After the third floor: the archives (see-through) → the meadow window (`MEADOW_SCREENS`; the landing is `LAND_AT` screens into it): the sky flips light, "THE OTHER SHORE" arrives with the landing and stays pinned (`--pin`, the camera is clamped), the tiger steps off the ladder a little left and forward onto the grass (`TIGER.glb.xGround`, `zGround`: the stage, clear of the pier and the rails; grass and flowers keep off it), turns to face you and sits; its gaze follows the cursor; "psst — click me" (not on phones, where it covered the button); clicking it, or the "Ask for a trick" button beside Say hi (`#trick`, the keyboard way in), opens the talent show (Dance / Zombie / Catwalk, `PERF` in `app.js`, only on the meadow; a group of buttons, Escape closes it and focus goes back) → then the footer paper. Clicking or tapping the tiger casts a ray from the click itself (window listener), so it works through the paper/window sections and on phones.
7. **Copy is truthful.** `content.js` holds only facts Kay verified. Where a project has a `detail.todo`, that field lists what is still missing — never invent roles, numbers, tools or outcomes. The project count drives every "thirteen" in the copy (`{N}` templates).
8. **Scope discipline.** Change only what Kay asks for. Before a layout-level change, restate what will and will not change and get a yes.

## Where things live (`merge/`)

- `content.js` — the single source of content and tuning (layout: `TITLE_SCREENS`, `INTRO_SCREENS`, `MEADOW_SCREENS`, `LAND_AT`; copy: `COPY.entry`, `COPY.intro.pages`, `COPY.talents`): `CATS`, `SIL` (placeholder SVG silhouettes), `PIECES` (13 projects; `img` = cut-out webp, `sil` = placeholder, `detail` = panel copy), `SECTIONS`, `VALUE_SCREENS`, `THEMES` (room skins: bg, fog, ink, wall texture, lights), `DECK` (the computer: screen z, NDC box per tier, entry/exit screens, bezel, colours), `COPY`, `ARCHIVE`, `RATE`, `CAMERA`, `TIGER` (glue, ledgeY, rung, step, scroll phases), `MOTION`, `SKY`, `LIGHT`.
- `app.js` — builds the DOM from content, the Léo text grammar, Lenis, the three.js world (pier + ladder, rooms with slabs and props per theme, ledges, meadow), the fallback tiger rig (`Rig` class: hips → pitch → torso/head/tail + four two-bone IK legs) and its scroll-scrubbed states, the GLB tiger (`loadTiger` / `setupTiger`: scales it to `TIGER.glb.height`, strips root motion, samples the climb clip for the ladder: rise per cycle, rung = half of it, rail width, depth, the rung under the left toe; `glbFrame`: clip times and weights per state and the root path; `headLook`: glance and look-back on the neck and head bones; `headBox`: the head's screen box, so the intro bubble and the talent menu sit beside it, never on the face; `perfPose` / `planWalk`: the talent show), the computer (`deckFrame()` → world rectangle of the glass, `PROPS.computer` → monitor/desk/keyboard/tower, the `#deck` overlay projection in `frame()`, its keys/wheel/drag handlers), the detail panel, layout tiers (`tune()`: phones shift the camera and glue the tiger lower), the frame loop.
- `style.css` — Léo's 12-column grid (20px gutter/gap), paper blocks, `.st` statements and `.st.title` section title bands, `.card` (frameless object + hover text), `.panel`, dark-window overrides, phone layout.
- `index.html` — authored as a fragment (title/meta/links + body); `preview.html` is the same wrapped as a full document for local viewing. Regenerate preview.html from index.html when index.html changes.
- `build.py` — bundles vendor + content + app + css into `dist/index.html` (single file; relative `assets/projects/*` paths) and `dist/artifact.html` (images inlined as data URIs, for claude.ai artifacts).
- `cutout.py` — photo → transparent cut-out (`<slug>.webp`, 960px) + ink silhouette (`<slug>-sil.webp`) with rembg. `python3 merge/cutout.py photo.jpg slug --crop x0,y0,x1,y1 --out assets/projects`.
- `tools/shot.js` — Playwright frame renderer for checking motion (see Workflow). `tools/errcheck.js` — loads the page and reports runtime errors. Both take `BASE=http://localhost:8000/preview.html` (the GLB only loads over http; `file://` shows the fallback rig).

## Workflow

- **Preview the source**: `cd merge && python3 -m http.server 8000` → http://localhost:8000/preview.html. `?snap=1` fixes the frame step (dt .2) for screenshots.
- **Build**: `cd merge && python3 build.py . dist`, then `cp dist/index.html ../index.html` (Pages serves the root). Commit both.
- **Check motion**: `npm i -D playwright && npx playwright install chromium` once, then `POS="0:h0,1.1:edge,4.3:climb,L:land,L0.7:sit" node merge/tools/shot.js` → `merge/shots/*.png` (`L` = the landing screen). `node merge/tools/errcheck.js` for console/page errors. Look at the frames — the tiger's limbs must move between neighbouring scroll positions; the camera must not turn.
- **Tiger GLB**: `B=/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender; G=assets/src/tiger/gestures; $B -b --factory-startup -P merge/tools/tiger_anim.py -- build "$G/_packs/Gestures Pack Basic/tiger-mixamo.fbx" $G assets/src/tiger/tiger-raw.glb`, then `npx gltf-transform resize … --width 1024 --height 1024`, `webp`, `resample`, `prune`, `dedup` → `assets/tiger/tiger.glb` (~1.2 MB). Tripo FBXs must be imported with image search off (their texture paths point at Tripo's server). A fresh build has Tripo's beige back again, so then paint it: `node merge/tools/tiger_tex.mjs extract assets/tiger/tiger.glb /tmp/tt`, `python3 merge/tools/tiger_paint.py /tmp/tt/mesh.json assets/src/tiger/tiger-mixamo-tex/tiger_basecolor.jpg /tmp/tt/paint.png`, `node merge/tools/tiger_tex.mjs swap assets/tiger/tiger.glb /tmp/tt/paint.png assets/tiger/tiger.glb`, and look at `VIEWS=climb node merge/tools/tiger-turntable.js assets/tiger/tiger.glb merge/shots/tiger.png`.
- **Cut-outs**: `pip3 install rembg onnxruntime pillow scipy opencv-python`, drop photos in `assets/projects/`, run `cutout.py`, then set `img: 'assets/projects/<slug>.webp'` on the piece in `content.js`.
- **Write-ups / the Studio**: Kay double-clicks `merge/studio/Open Studio.command` (or `python3 merge/studio/server.py`) → http://localhost:8010. She edits and says "studio updated"; Claude starts each round with `python3 merge/studio/claude.py changes` (her edits vs the last look, answers, notes), acts (edit the JSON files directly — the Studio notices within 4 s), then `claude.py mark`. Every fact in a write-up cites a Drive source; what isn't in a source becomes a question in the block, never a sentence. Research drafts land in `writeups-private/drafts/` and come in with `claude.py import <slug>` (won't replace blocks Kay touched). **Going live**: Kay sets a project to ● Approved in the Studio → `writeups.js` updates → build → commit → push (only the approved project's JSON and its pictures, never `writeups-private/`, `data/profile.json` or client material she hasn't cleared).
- **The Drive**: Kay's `KAY-PORTFOLIO` folder is mounted at `~/Library/CloudStorage/GoogleDrive-kaytu2027@u.northwestern.edu/My Drive/KAY-PORTFOLIO` (shortcuts to course team folders resolve with `find -L`). `.gdoc`/`.gslides`/`.gsheet` files are stubs holding a `doc_id` → read them with the Drive MCP `read_file_content`. PDFs/HEIC/pptx/videos are real files (`merge/tools/pics.py`). No people in pictures (never patients/users/minors).
- **Deploy**: commit to `main` and `git push origin main`; Pages rebuilds in about a minute. Kay pushes herself (SSH key).
- Commit messages: end with a `Co-Authored-By: Claude …` line.
